The _depositar_ team participated in and contributed to the [International Data Week 2025 (IDW 2025)](https://idw2025.org/), held from October 13 to 16, in Brisbane, Australia. 

Hilary Shiue and Tyng-Ruey Chuang organized a session on [Research data stewardship in the Asia Pacific – What is happening now and how to move forward?](https://scidatacon.org/event/9/contributions/34/) which included four presentations from research teams in Japan, Singapore, and Taiwan. In the session, we talked about [Building Sustainable Research Data Services in Taiwan: Experience from the depositar](https://pid.depositar.io/ark:37281/k5m5h1k0k). 

With Christine Kirkpatrick (San Diego Supercomputer Center), Tyng-Ruey Chuang presented [The Role of Data In AI for Science](https://pid.depositar.io/ark:37281/k5t1z2r3g) as part of [AI for Science — CODATA Convened Session](https://scidatacon.org/event/9/contributions/233/).

The International Data Week 2025 were organized by [CODATA](https://codata.org/), [World Data System](https://worlddatasystem.org/), and [Research Data Alliance](https://www.rd-alliance.org/). The four-day conference has been hosted and supported by the [Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC)](https://ardc.edu.au/). [The conference program](https://idw2025.org/program-at-a-glance/) consisted of 107 sessions and 4 plenaries, as well as 130 posters. [More than 800 delegates from 75 countries were represented](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lauren-o-keeffe123_idw2025-australia-data-activity-7385892419097018368-_MKA).

[The next edition of International Data Week (IDW 2027)](https://internationaldataweek.org/idw-2027/) will take place in Cape Town, South Africa, on September 20 to 23, 2027.
