The SciPy Conference is an annual event exploring the application of Python in open-source scientific research. This year, [SciPy 2026](https://www.scipy2026.scipy.org/) was held from July 13 to 19 in Minneapolis, USA. Marking the 25th anniversary of the SciPy Conference, the event themed "Spirit of SciPy" focused on "Data-Driven Discovery, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence," promoting the implementation and breakthroughs of AI technology across diverse scientific fields such as earth sciences, biology, medicine, astronomy, and physics.

Yu-Lin Chen and Cheng-Jen Lee from the depositar team presented a poster titled "Toward Reliable Localization of Free and Open Source Software: LLM-assisted Translation Workflows for QGIS," sharing their recent [experience in using Large Language Models to supplement the Traditional Chinese interface of QGIS](/news/260210/). The full paper will be published in the [SciPy 2026 online proceedings](https://proceedings.scipy.org/) after peer review.
