After running the Research Data Management Hub (RDM Hub) on a Drupal website for many years, we have now completed the migration to a new static site built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and [Sveltia CMS](https://github.com/sveltia/sveltia-cms), using the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme. The new site offers a cleaner structure, improved performance, and a more maintainable workflow for our team. 

This update was led by Ting-Yu Chuang, with valuable feedback and support from the depositar team throughout the transition. We invite you to explore the new [RDM Hub](https://rdm.depositar.io) and follow future news and updates there!
